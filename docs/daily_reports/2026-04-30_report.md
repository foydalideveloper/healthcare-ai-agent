### 5. Pipeline improvements applied

**Frame sampling: 8 → 12 frames, front-weighted**
- Previous: 8 evenly-spaced frames across the full video
- New: 12 frames — first 40% of video gets 60% of frames (7 frames)
- Rationale: user introduces items in the first 3-5 seconds; even sampling spends too many frames on empty background at the end

**Visual exercise detection in Gemma/Nemotron prompts**
- Added explicit rule: detect physical activity in frames (running, stretching, weights)
- Short-clip rule: "3-5 second frame showing someone holding a snack is a valid food event"
- Both Gemma and Nemotron prompts updated

**Exercise timestamp extraction (Haiku)**
- New `event_time` field in Haiku SYSTEM_PROMPT exercises schema
- Homophone correction added: "tea of this" → "THREE of this", etc.
- `to_legacy_format()` now threads `event_time` through
- `process_video()` backfills `user_activity_event.detected_at` when user states a past time
- Dashboard now shows "9:00 AM" instead of "1:00 PM" for morning exercise logged in afternoon
